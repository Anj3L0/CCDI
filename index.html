About CCDI EDU

A centralized school information and communication website proposed for Computer Communication Development Institute.

Purpose

CCDI EDU brings announcements, schedules, events, tuition information, enrollment requirements, notifications, and other school-related information into one accessible platform.

Designed for

Students, professors, and authorized school administrators/staff, with role-based access to the functions needed by each user.
